# Hotel service corridor

This rare variation draws on the early-century hotel halls, numbered doors, and electric lamp hum described by the [Wikidot Level 5 entry](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-5). It is an original interpretation within this game's fluorescent world, not a claim that the different Backrooms canons share one hotel. Geometry, room numbers, and textures are original; no wiki prose, photographs, or branded signs are copied.

One quarter of existing three-section corridor runs receive the same seeded choice. Their cell arrays, gates, ceilings, and cadence remain unchanged. Joinery occupies only closed wall bays: cream upper walls, light wooden dado, framed doors and transoms, brass numbers, and one small key cupboard in the middle section. Existing fluorescent fixtures provide all lighting.

All numbers share one 512×256 atlas per material library, lettered only after the installed Geist Mono face loads. Late font completion cannot revive disposed resources. Door geometry joins normal material batches; number plates form one additional mesh per section. Even a hypothetical section with all 24 side bays closed and a cupboard stays below 4,500 triangles and eight material batches.
