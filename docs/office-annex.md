# Abandoned office annex

A rare variation of the lobby: a low fluorescent ceiling, bare carpeted floor,
sealed interior windows with cream blinds, one drinking-water station and an
unattended vending cabinet. The scale, layout, models and details are original.
There is no natural daylight, combat encounter or new interaction to explain.

Research reference: [Level 4 — Abandoned Office](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-4),
Backrooms Wikidot, attributed there to u/M654zy, CC BY-SA 3.0; accessed
2026-09-22. That page describes empty offices, suspicious windows and scattered
water dispensers. It is currently marked for rewriting. This room takes those
broad architectural ideas as inspiration, rather than claiming to reproduce a
canonical level; Wikidot, Fandom and the Kane Pixels films are distinct versions
of the setting. No source prose, imagery, insignia or audio is copied.

One third of eligible lobby slots use the annex, chosen by tape seed and global
section coordinate independently of regeneration depth. Lobby/corridor/pool/food
court cadence and the rare party/courtyard/street selection remain intact.
Redundant perimeter openings close only after a connectivity check, with all
four approach directions retained. Fixtures sit against closed walls, less than
one meter deep; the room center and every doorway remain empty. Geometry uses
existing shared materials and batching, with no new textures, lights or assets.
