# Warehouse annex

A rare food-court slot becomes an empty loading annex with a smooth warm floor,
chunky columns, overhead service ducts, and a few pallets of sealed cartons.
The selector uses the tape seed and section coordinates; returning to an evicted
section or descending at the same coordinates preserves its room identity.
The normal food-court kind, footprint, lighting, acoustic hard floor, and maze
boundary gates remain unchanged.

## Research and adaptation

Primary reference: [Level 1 — The Habitable Zone](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-1),
by Praetor3005 and DivineAtlas, accessed 2026-09-22. Its Halls and Aquila/Gild
sector descriptions establish broad industrial interiors, structural columns,
ceiling services and supplies. This annex adapts those spatial ideas into
original procedural geometry. It is an atmospheric variation, not a claim that
this game's tape descent implements the wiki's numbered-level progression.

The adaptation keeps the game's bright fluorescent illumination and warm cream
palette. It does not reproduce the reference's blackouts, hostile darkness,
settlements, logos, anomalous resources, text, images or recordings. Pallets are
scenery, not promised interactable loot. All geometry uses the project's existing
materials and disposal path; no external assets or new texture downloads.
